# API breakthrough deck

> **Status:** Not generated. Build this only after the breadth-first waves and
> the first atlas pass.

This will be the primary human entry point: a deliberately varied selection of
roughly 10–15 surprising, high-leverage ideas or fragments. Selection is for
novelty, conceptual leverage, and diversity—not an assertion that these are the
most feasible or “best” APIs.

## Card shape

Each card should fit roughly on one screen and contain:

- a short name and the originating raw seed identifiers;
- the smallest useful sketch of the idea;
- the mechanism that makes it different;
- what it might unlock;
- its largest unanswered question; and
- a place to record the human response and follow-up request.
